# CRSIS-47 — The Five Pearls

Status: Owner-defined institutional symbolism; preserved for design provenance. Not a change to the research methodology, not a publication approval.

The five pearls on the lower ring of the CRSIS-47 Evidence Chamber shield represent the institution's **unwritten principles**, in the owner's stated order:

1. **Time**
2. **Affection**
3. **Patience**
4. **Love**
5. **If need be, my life for the truth**

Owner's statement (preserved in substance): “THE 5 PREALS REPRESENT THE UNWRITEN PRINCIPAL OF OR INSTATUTION TIME AFFECTION PATIANCE LOVE AND IF NEED BE MY LIFE FOR THE TRUTH.”

## Design controls
- Display **exactly five visually distinct pearls** on the shield, not three or four.
- Retain the gold star, stemless acorn cap, flame ring, chamber, lamp, open book, T+47, and institutional name **CRSIS-47**.
- The pearls are symbolic, not a request to print the principles around the shield. Do not alter the four existing ring labels (EVIDENCE, ASSESSMENT, DECISION, RESPONSE) to accommodate the principles.
- Preserve the owner-selected dramatic navy-and-gold closing composition, motto and bottom navigation. Header uses a small shield; final site section uses the large closing composition.
- The five principles are not experimental claims or modifications to the frozen CRSIS-47 T+47 methodology.
- Any future graphical revisions must be compared with the owner-selected visual reference and reviewed before publication. No changes to the live website are authorized by this record.
