CRSIS-47 Research Glossary

Selected source-verified terminology. Research constructs and proposed methods are not claims of empirical validation, accreditation, or experimental authorization.

60 editorially filtered entries · v0.4 review edition

CRSIS-47

Expansion: Cyber Risk Security Intelligence System Named system, distinct from PGDT and T+47 Theory.

PGDT

Expansion: People, Governance, Data, Technology Proposed original CRSIS-47 research framework/analytical lens, attributed to the research owner in L005 and described as exploratory in L006. Distinct from the CRSIS-47 system and T+47 Theory; empirical validation and independent originality priority are not established.

T+47 Theory

Owner-proposed theory requiring explicit propositions, operational definitions and empirical examination.

T+0

First scheduled benchmark evidence release to the evaluated system; scenario-clock origin, not intrusion onset, analyst file-opening time or processing start.

T+47

Final scheduled benchmark checkpoint, 47 simulated seconds from T+0; not a wait or wall-clock SLA.

Sub-47

First joint qualifying malicious crossing strictly before T+47.

TTCD

Expansion: Time to Confident Detection The earliest scheduled scenario checkpoint at which the system simultaneously classifies the scenario as MALICIOUS and reports an intrusion-confidence estimate of at least 80%. Scenario clock starts at T+0, the first scheduled benchmark evidence release. Schedule: T+0, T+10, T+20, T+30, T+40, T+47. No crossing through T+47 means a threshold-qualified detection miss and undefined TTCD. BENIGN reference cases meeting the joint threshold are false escalations, not successful detections. Confidence is not automatically calibrated probability.

threshold-qualified malicious detection

MALICIOUS and ≥80% at same scheduled checkpoint; neither alone suffices.

scenario time

Protected simulated evidence-release clock, separate from execution latency.

checkpoint assessment

Assessment uses only cumulative evidence eligible at scheduled checkpoint.

progressive evidence

Cumulative benchmark schedule: T+0/T+10/T+20/T+30/T+40/T+47.

MALICIOUS

Nominal assessment label; detection also requires confidence threshold and malicious reference scenario.

BENIGN

Nominal assessment label; Case 004 non-malicious failure may still be harmful.

UNCERTAIN

Explicit assessment state, not silently recoded into BENIGN or MALICIOUS.

ground truth

Protected MALICIOUS/BENIGN reference class used in scoring, not revealed to evaluated system.

reported confidence estimate

System-reported experimental estimate; not presumed calibrated compromise probability.

AI

Expansion: Artificial Intelligence Pattern detection, decision support and recommended actions in SRS.

API

Expansion: Application Programming Interface Software-to-software data exchange.

EDR

Expansion: Endpoint Detection and Response Endpoint telemetry and approved containment integration.

IAM

Expansion: Identity and Access Management Identity/authentication integration.

SIEM

Expansion: Security Information and Event Management Log/alert aggregation and correlation integration.

SOAR

Expansion: Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response Incident playbooks, response workflows and evidence tracking.

MFA

Expansion: Multi-Factor Authentication Adaptive/step-up authentication control in the SRS; exact expansion is explicit in S016 glossary.

ATT&CK

Expansion: Adversarial Tactics, Techniques & Common Knowledge MITRE knowledge model of adversary behavior used for threat/technique mapping; mapping alone does not validate a detector.

D3FEND

Expansion: Detection, Denial, and Disruption Framework Empowering Network Defense MITRE cybersecurity countermeasure knowledge graph supporting defensive vocabulary and relationships; it does not by itself prioritize or establish countermeasure effectiveness.

ATLAS

Expansion: Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems MITRE AI threat/testing reference used in proposed architecture.

NIST

Expansion: National Institute of Standards and Technology Standards organization referenced for governance/control alignment.

CSF

Expansion: Cybersecurity Framework NIST CSF 2.0 governance/security alignment reference.

AI RMF

Expansion: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework NIST voluntary AI risk-management framework; CRSIS-47 SRS references version 1.0.

RMF

Expansion: Risk Management Framework NIST risk-management process for organizational and system information-security/privacy risk; framework reference, not evidence of CRSIS-47 validation.

NIST AI 600-1

Expansion: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile NIST generative-AI companion profile, published July 26, 2024; governance alignment reference in SRS.

OWASP LLM

Expansion: OWASP = Open Worldwide Application Security Project; LLM = large language model Compound shorthand for OWASP large-language-model application-security guidance; not a single official standard title.

Zero Trust

Security model of continuous verification, least privilege and explicit authorization.

IEEE 830

Referenced requirements-document guidance; S016 states IEEE 830-style.

SRS

Expansion: System Requirements Specification Project-document sense explicitly defined by L004 §1.3; preserve original document titles, including singular Requirement variant. Separate IEEE software-specification sense is recorded in the distinct-sense register, not treated as identical.

cyber-resilience digital twin

Proposed model linking identities, sessions, assets, controls and risk for attack-path/containment simulation.

telemetry

Security observations supplying proposed identity-centric reasoning.

graph-based attack reasoning

Proposed attack interpretation through entity relationships.

privacy-preserving machine learning

Proposed learning feature, not demonstrated deployment.

human-governed autonomous response

Proposed response subject to human governance; no execution authorization follows from SRS.

audit receipt

Linked integrity record; local chain does not establish independent immutable custody.

idempotent replay

Repeated existing-event behavior tested; not proof of competing unique-write stress.

crash consistency

Recovery behavior under specified faults, not universal power-loss durability.

ARB

Expansion: Academic Review Board Institutional readiness review; October 2 decision Approved with Minor Revisions.

IRB

Expansion: Institutional Review Board Institutional clearance/determination process required under L003 before prospective study activity; L011/L012 are readiness drafts and do not record clearance.

Brier score

Checkpoint mean of (p_ij − Y_i)^2, with p on [0,1] and binary protected reference Y. Lower squared prediction error does not independently establish calibration.

IPA

Expansion: Index of Prediction Accuracy 1 − BS_model/BS_null; supplement to raw Brier and direct calibration.

calibration

Validation property assessed separately from confidence trajectories/Brier.

calibration intercept

Calibration diagnostic; controlling SAP gives ideal intercept 0, separate from slope and graphical assessment.

calibration slope

Calibration diagnostic; controlling SAP gives ideal slope 1 and requires sufficient sample and inspection of calibration relationship.

internal prevalence-based reference forecast

Reference derived from validation-set class balance, not real-world base rate.

multinomial mixed-effects model

Primary candidate framework for nominal repeated categorical outcomes.

subjective teaching confidence

Optional classroom estimate; not demonstrated calibration or research measurement.

independent reviewer disposition

Response to review; does not itself assert independent acceptance/release.

FORTRESS America

Documentary national-resilience case, not evidence of CRSIS-47 adoption/effectiveness.

RCA

Expansion: Root Cause Analysis Incident-cause investigation/report term explicitly expanded in CrowdStrike executive summary p.1; vendor findings remain attributed.

Professional / Leadership Edition

Self-directed/team development edition; not certification/accreditation.

Instructor / Facilitator Edition

Professional content plus facilitated workshop, rubric, responses and debrief.

SOC

Expansion: Security Operations Center Security operations context; separate SOC response latency from scenario TTCD.

trusted evidence layer

Future system layer preserving verifiable evidence, threat intelligence and response records.
